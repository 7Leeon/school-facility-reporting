Algorithm 1: Matching

Each available staff member gets a score from 0 to 100: 50% skill in the category, 30% free capacity, 20% zone closeness. The highest score is assigned. Staff at their workload limit or marked inactive are skipped. With no match, the issue stays Pending and is matched again, most urgent first, when capacity frees up.

Algorithm 2: Status tracking

A state machine: Pending, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, Closed. Only valid moves are allowed, a Resolved issue can be reopened, every change is time-stamped, and an urgency deadline (4h, 24h, 72h, 120h) flags overdue work.
